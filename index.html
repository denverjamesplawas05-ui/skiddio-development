Everything in one place

Less app-switching, less manual re-planning.

📋

Checklist first

A simple to-do list is the default view, because it's what most students told us they want. Add a task with a date, start and end time and you're done.

✨

One-tap Rescheduler

When the day slips, press Rescheduler. Skiddio finds overdue tasks and moves each one into the next free slot that fits, keeping a buffer between tasks.

📅

Drag-and-drop calendar

See your plan by month, week or day. Drag a schedule to a new time, and if it lands on another one, choose whether to swap them or replace the old one.

☁️

Saved to your account

Sign in and your tasks, plus your working hours and buffer settings, are saved to your account. Dark mode and a phone-friendly layout come built in.

How it works

Three steps to a calmer day.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up with your email, then set your working hours and the buffer you want between tasks.

  2. 2

    Add your tasks

    Give each task a date, start and end time. It appears in your checklist and on your calendar straight away.

  3. 3

    Fix slips in one tap

    Press Rescheduler and moved tasks are highlighted with their new time, or drag things around the calendar yourself.
